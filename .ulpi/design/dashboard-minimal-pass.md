# Dashboard minimal pass (18 Nov 2026)

Status: implemented in the working tree (uncommitted). Owner decisions: Pixl's system architecture with Stasis's layout shapes; fewer boxed buttons, more clickable text; calmer Ledger and Shop; richer new-project page. Analysis: `internal/reports/ui-system-analysis-and-rating-2026-11-18.md` and `internal/reports/minimal-ui-direction-2026-11-18.md`.

## What changed

- `app/_components/platform/minimal.css` (imported by `PlatformShell.tsx`): all new rules, selectors start `html .pf` so they win over older rules.
- Small text tiers raised one step in the dashboard CSS files (0.6875 to 0.75em, 0.75 to 0.8125em, 0.8125 to 0.875em; 11px dot labels to 12px). Reading text stays 16px.
- Filters, sort and tabs (`.pf-explore-filters`, used by Shop tags, Explore, Orders and others) are text with a 2px underline for the current one. Hit area stays 44px, focus ring unchanged, `aria-current` unchanged.
- Secondary actions in dialogs and the wizard (Cancel, Close, Back, More ideas, Undo, Stay) read as text; the one primary action stays a button.
- Shop discount list stays a list (tests rely on it) but is one quiet line under the tag tabs.
- Thin themed scrollbars on all platform scroll areas.
- Dialog headers: a ruler along the head; danger dialogs get hatching and a red rule.
- Ledger: large balance number, hatched strip for pending, Earned and Spent as two figures, borderless rows with a hatched strip on pending rows, text status, one-line reasons, text pager.
- REVERTED on owner request (18 Nov 2026): the New project page is back to its earlier layout and styles. The item below is no longer in the code.
- New project page: two columns on wide screens with a live preview card and a descending step readout (labelled as step progress, not hours), pixel icons on the kind tiles, registration marks that open on hover, focus and selection, progress segments that fill, staggered step content. Below 1000px the preview shows only on the name step.

## States and accessibility

Reduced motion and paused motion turn every animation off. Forced colors: hatching and marks fall back to system colours. Text controls keep a 44px hit area, visible focus, and `aria-current`. Dialogs are unchanged native `<dialog>` with the same focus handling.

## Not done in this pass

Orders redesign (tracker in list rows), Profile, Community and Settings restructuring into tabs, Shop tab grouping, hardware pre-flight checklist and starter guides (need owner content), deleting the unused `JournalDialog.tsx`, light theme and tablet checks.

## Kind tiles (new project, step 1), 18 Nov 2026

Skills used: project-frontend routing, emilkowal-animations (ease-out `--pf-ease`, 100 to 260ms, press scale 0.98, hover gated by `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, nothing animates under reduced motion). No new sprite art; the tiles reuse `terminal.png` and `cpu.png`.

Two tall tiles (min height 360px, 260px on phones). Hover, keyboard focus or selection: the tile lifts 8px onto a hard shadow, registration marks open, a two-line fact sheet slides up, and for a mouse a lamp reveals hidden art (code or a circuit) in the top half. Touch has no hover, so the facts stay visible and the art is off. Forced colors: marks and art are hidden, the selected tile gets a thicker border. The art and fact sheet are `aria-hidden`; the radio's accessible name is unchanged. Facts reuse existing wording (tracks, Hackatime for software, Lapse for hardware). The earlier two-column preview and depth readout stay removed.
