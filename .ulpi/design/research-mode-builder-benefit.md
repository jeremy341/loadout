# Research Mode builder benefit

## Decision

Research Mode is an optional modifier that can be added to any of the four LOADOUT tracks. A builder who publishes a clear, reproducible technical report alongside their project can earn bonus Track XP.

## Builder value

The bonus Track XP helps a builder reach higher levels sooner. Higher levels improve their discount on eligible prizes from that track, so Research Mode rewards builders who turn a technical question into useful, documented evidence.

## Guardrails

- Research Mode is not a fifth track.
- The bonus is earned through a reproducible report, not simply by selecting the modifier.
- The exact bonus amount and review criteria remain to be defined before launch.
- The project still belongs to the track or tracks represented by its technical work.
