## Product Purpose

LOADOUT is being prepared as a technical builder YSWS. Its public site explains what kinds of tools, systems, compute, and hardware projects fit, how shipped work relates to progression, and what a visitor can do next when a destination is confirmed.

## Positioning

LOADOUT focuses on work that expands technical capability for the builder or other builders. It is narrower than a general build-anything program. The defining loop connects shipped artifacts to a persistent Digital Loadout and rewards to a Physical Loadout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The four tracks are Tools, Systems, Compute, and Hardware. Research Mode is a project modifier, not a track.
- Bolts are global spendable currency. Track XP is per-track progression.
- Shipped work can become part of the Digital Loadout; rewards upgrade the Physical Loadout.
- The quality rubric is Originality, Technical Depth, Execution, and Documentation.
- Do not claim program acceptance, dates, eligibility, sponsors, participation numbers, reward stock, exact prices/discounts, or join/login destinations without confirmed data.
- External actions and catalogue details must remain absent when their destinations or values are not configured.
- The quality assessment may inform the planned Bolt multiplier; no public numeric formula is approved in this page scope.
