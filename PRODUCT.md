# Product context

## Platform and audience

LOADOUT is a technical-builder YSWS for high-school students aged 13 to 18. Hack Club teen verification is required; the account integration and operational approval remain to be confirmed. The homepage and public Docs use the 13–18 age policy. Prize-delivery regions and operating dates remain open.

## Purpose and positioning

LOADOUT focuses on technical work that expands capability for the builder or other builders. Its four tracks are Tools, Systems, Compute, and Hardware. Research Mode is a project modifier, not a fifth track. The intended progression connects shipped work to a Digital Loadout and planned rewards to a Physical Loadout.

The four quality dimensions are Originality, Technical Depth, Execution, and Documentation; the team uses them to guide its accept-or-reject reason, with no score. Ore is global spendable currency; Track XP records non-spendable progress by track. Every approved hour earns 10 Ore, with no multiplier. Fifteen lifetime levels end at LV.15 with 3,500 XP, or about 350 solid-work hours in one track. Permanent track discounts reach 15% at LV.15. Field Requisitions at LV.5, LV.8, LV.11 and LV.15 each provide one use of 10% off a matching prize's base price, for prizes from 400 Ore, on top of the level discount. Combined savings are capped at 25% of the base price. Plan 02 owns the full status matrix. Exact XP equations, intermediate curves, Usage Milestone thresholds, referral rewards, General Requisition triggers, Era bonus stacking, and Custom Order values remain open.

## Current product boundary

`apps/landing` is a public explanation of a program draft. It does not implement participant accounts, submissions, review, social features, a live economy, orders, fulfillment, or event management. The selected future platform direction includes public open-source project pages and journals; Hackatime for software, Lapse for Hardware, eight-hour journal updates; guided missions, Seasons, quests, achievements, streaks, leaderboards, Community Eras and rewards; friends, follows, likes, post notifications, comments, project discovery, a public idea board, upvotes and collectibles; event RSVPs and certificates; and a shop wishlist. Referrals, verified-user Usage Milestones, General Requisitions and a one-time +50 Ore achievement for the first meaningful external contribution are also planned, with their open thresholds and reward triggers documented in Plans 02 and the future-platform proposal. LOADOUT IRL is future work. The external RSVP destination expresses interest and does not establish enrollment or program acceptance.

Owner-approved draft prices and discounts may be shown only when clearly labelled provisional; unresolved values cannot be presented as final. Program acceptance, live eligibility, dates, sponsors, participation counts, stock, ordering, fulfillment, service availability and join/login destinations require confirmed evidence.
