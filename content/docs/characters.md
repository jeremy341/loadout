*Orry and Goose Lee are characters and workers from LOADOUT. This is how a miner who wanted a fortune ended up working with a duck who wanted an adventure.*

## Orry, the Ruhr miner {#orry}

Orry grew up poor in the Ruhrgebiet of Germany, and the other children made sure he knew it. He couldn't do much about their taunts then, but he carried the shame with him into adulthood. By the time he became a miner, he had decided that by getting rich he would put an end to being looked down upon and win the respect he deserved.

Hannes, an older miner, was one of the first people in his life which really helped him when he had nothing. He taught Orry the work and looked after him, becoming his mentor and closest friend. Under Hannes, Orry learnt to mark and make safe routes and leave useful tools behind for whoever came next. Even as he worked toward a fortune of his own, he remembered what it had meant for somebody to help him.

Eventually, the fortune came. Orry had money, a private stash of ore to admire and a close circle offriends he was happy to share with. Though People outside that circle usually got a little more grumbling before he gave anything away. He could afford to be generous now and he was, but he still hated being made to feel small and this can be seen. A question about his wealth could set off a boast, and praise for another miner sent him straight back to work, determined to do better.

Goose Lee in particular had a talent for getting under his skin. Their trouble began at a swimming pool when they met in their early twenties, where Orry fell for a girl and learnt to swim to impress her. She preferred Goose. Nothing came of the crush, but Orry kept swimming and eventually became an Olympic champion in the Games for moles. Though nowadays, he likes to say he'd known he was a natural all along. Of course that isnt true, but it is still a better story than admitting he was rejected by a girl and had to work hard to get good at something.

He hadn't forgotten the duck, though. When Orry arrived for his first day at LOADOUT and found Goose already working there, he considered leaving, but Jerry's salary offer was too good to give up, so he stayed whih he really regrets to this day. Soon the old grudge had found a new way to show op in their mining competitions. Whenever Goose beat him, Orry grumbled, trained harder and prepared for the next rematch.

## Goose Lee, the adventurous duck {#goose-lee}

Despite his name, Goose Lee is a duck.

Goose had come to Germany with much less to prove. He'd grown up in a comfortable Chinese-American family in the United States, done well at school and been popular his whole life. His parents expected university but Goose wanted an adventure, and when Pond university turned him down, he went looking for adventure in the German mines. If it didn't work out, home was still there, and he could always apply again for university.

His family was surname was Li. He'd chosen to name himeslf “Goose Lee” after Bruce Lee, whose videos he watched and whose moves he tried to copy. Sharing his hero's Asian background he had somehow convinced himself that he shared the talent too. Which of course wasnt truem  In practice, Goose was trash and had very little skill, but plenty of confidence which borders arrogance. Most people let him show off cause they liked his personality. Orry tried telling him the truth, then gave up when he realised Goose thought the criticism was another joke.

Joking was how Goose handled most uncomfortable things. If he failed, he blamed his luck and stopped trying, if a friend was upset, he tried to make them laugh, sometimes without even noticing he was making it worse. He wanted to be liked and assumed the people around him understood that.

At the swimming pool, he hadn't even known Orry was interested in the girl. She had liked Goose, but he'd turned her down. Years later, he still thought of the competitions between hin and Orry as friendly banter. He couldn't see why the mole took them so seriously, while Orry couldn't understand how Goose could treat the mines as an adventure when he had a comfortable life waiting at home, and people only come to them wheny they are desperate.

Goose did respect one thing about prry though: when his friends needed help, Orry could set aside his own grief and greed to be there for them. He had never told Orry that. The teasing was much easier, and it was what Orry heard instead, always heard instead.

## The mine accident {#mine-accident}

Their rivalry stopped being a game when Goose made a dangerous mistake that caused a fatal accident in the mine. Orry put himself at risk to get him out. In that moment, whatever layed between them mattered less than saving his colleague.

After the rescue, Goose had to reconsider the miner he'd been looking down on. Orry's strength of mind and character had surprised him, but admitting that aloud was harder than making another joke. What he managed was, “I guess you're right, I'm sorry about that.”

Orry heard the hesitation. After risking himself for Goose, he was baffled and couldnt believe his reaction, he wanted respect and gratitude, and what did he get an apolofy which gave him neither. Goose became quieter and less playful, but that change didn't tell Orry what he wanted to hear. Both had been affected by the accident, neither knew how to speak to the other about it and this caused orrys resentment to build up even further.

## Their next adventure {#treasure-hunt}

A lead to hidden treasure gave them a reason to work together again. Goose wanted the danger and adventure of the search. Orry wanted the fortune at the end of it, This was enough for them to agree to work together one las time.
