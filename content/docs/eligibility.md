LOADOUT is for high-school students aged **13 to 18**. You don't need to belong to a club or know an organizer to take part. Before you plan a build around the program, though, check the launch status below: we haven't opened submissions or orders yet.

## Who can take part in this GREAT EVENT? {#who}

Anyone, regardless of their experience level. You can be new to building or already have several projects behind you. What matters at review is the work you submit and the evidence behind it.

 As for age and shipping, we will need to confirm age verification and check which countries we can deliver prizes to. We'll publish those details before launch so you can check your eligibility before committing time to an expected reward.

## Where do things stand right now? {#where-things-stand}

**LOADOUT hasn't opened.** Hack Club acceptance, funding and a start date aren't even confirmed. This website explains the program we're putting together; it doesn't yet have participant accounts, submissions, reviews or orders.

Some rules are settled, while others still need a decision:

| Topic | Decided | Still to finish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Progress | Four lifetime tracks. LV.15 is about 350 hours of solid work in one track. | The XP equation, intermediate level thresholds and Research bonus. |
| Discounts | Up to 20% from your track level, plus one 20% Requisition. Five tickets per track at levels 3/6/9/12/15; eligible prizes start at 400 Ore. | Intermediate discounts and Custom Order tiers. |
| Building | No cap on real hours or projects. A repeat ship needs at least five new approved hours. | Account, tracking and submission integrations. |
| Prizes | Eight examples with working Ore prices. | Final catalog, stock, delivery and tax terms. |
| Eras | 10% extra Ore for qualifying projects. | Era Points rules, themes and targets. |
| Launch and IRL | The online program and a three-day Ruhr event are being developed. | Acceptance, funding and dates; the event's venue and registration. |

The working prices and milestones may be tuned before launch. They're useful for understanding the program, but listing an item doesn't reserve stock for you.

## What does RSVP do?

[RSVP](https://rsvp.soon.it/loadout) puts you on the interest list for updates. It helps us see how many people want to build through LOADOUT. You'll still need to register when the program opens, and an RSVP doesn't reserve a prize or an IRL place.

## Which rules can I rely on?

These guides describe the current program draft. Where a missing decision affects what you can do, the relevant guide will say so. The [IRL page](/docs/irl#status) is where we'll publish event details.

Found two pages that disagree? Send both links to [#loadout-help](https://app.slack.com/client/E09V59WQY1E/C0C6XBLM0M7), or open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/jeremy341/loadout). It helps us fix the actual sentence instead of leaving you to guess which version to trust.
