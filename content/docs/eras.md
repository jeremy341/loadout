An Era gives the community a shared technical theme. You can build something around it, help move the shared progress forward and earn an Ore bonus by marking your project as an Era project when you submit it. If you're already working on something else, you can carry on with the usual review and rewards, though you will miss out on your 10% Ore bonus.

## What an Era is {#shared}

The same theme can lead to very different projects. For a theme about the physical world, one builder might design a sensor board while another writes a service that collects readings or a tool to visualize them. Each is working in their own track, with a reason to compare ideas and help the others.

Approved Era projects add **Era Points** to a community total. One approved hour adds one point, and only once a project has passed every review stage and been approved. That total moves everyone toward the next theme. The points belong to shared progress. You can't spend them in the shop, though you still benefit from the community's overall progress.

## How do I mark a project? {#qualify}

Tick the Era box when you submit your project. Any project can be marked, and you don't need to match a specific objective. Marking a project doesn't affect whether the ship is accepted.

For an illustrative theme about electricity, ideas might include measuring physical data, controlling a device, automating a process or improving efficiency. Those are examples of how a theme could work, not an announced set of challenges.

## The Era bonus {#bonus}

A marked project earns **10% extra Ore** on its base award, once per project, when it is finally approved. If its approved hours earn 720 Ore, the bonus adds 72, for a total of 792 Ore. Resubmitting after a "needs changes" decision doesn't pay the bonus again.

The bonus doesn't add Track XP or change the four quality dimensions. How it would combine with future Ore bonuses remains undecided.

## How the community moves on {#advance}

Each Era has a points target and stays open for a minimum time. Meeting only one of those conditions doesn't end it. Once both are met, the Era is ready to advance and the owner confirms the move to the next one. Nothing advances on a timer.

Points count toward the Era that is current when your project is approved, and nothing carries over: each Era starts at zero. A finished Era keeps the total it closed with. If someone is banned, their points leave the current Era. Themes, targets and point values haven't been published yet.

## Eras and your own levels {#xp}

When the theme changes, you keep your Ore, Track XP, levels and accepted projects. You can follow the new objective or continue the project you were already building. [Ore, XP & levels](/docs/progress) explains that lifetime progress.

## How long an Era runs {#seasons}

An Era runs for at least its minimum time and then until the owner confirms the next one. Your personal progress carries through every Era, so a new theme won't reset the levels you've earned.

We haven't announced a sequence of named Eras or launch dates.
