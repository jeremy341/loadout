Community Eras give builders a shared technical theme to explore. Approved projects help the community advance; an Era is not a new track or a spendable balance.

## Shared progress {#shared}

Approved, non-duplicate work contributes Era Points. Those points belong to shared Era progress and cannot be bought, transferred, or spent.

Optional objectives can inspire work across the four tracks. A project still follows normal fit, evidence, authorship, and quality review. Not matching the theme does not make an otherwise valid project invalid.

## Moving to the next Era {#advance}

The intended sequence is **approved projects → shared Era Points → community target and minimum duration met → advancement at an eligible weekly reset**.

Reaching the target early does not immediately start the next Era. If the target has not been met, the community keeps working toward it.

Themes, point calculations, thresholds, and schedules are not published as live configuration. This site does not display an active Era or a progress meter.

## A planned project bonus {#bonus}

A reviewer separately checks whether a project meaningfully matches the active objectives. A qualifying project is intended to receive a separate Bolt bonus, not extra Track XP.

The public bonus configuration remains pending. Era relevance does not replace any of the four quality dimensions.

## Seasons are different {#seasons}

| Layer | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Tracks | Your lifetime technical progress. |
| Eras | Shared technical themes and community advancement. |
| Seasons | Competitive periods and rankings. |

Accepted projects and lifetime Track XP stay with you across both Era changes and Seasons. No sequence of named Eras or launch dates is promised here.
