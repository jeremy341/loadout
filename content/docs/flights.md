> **Provisional.** The in-person event is not confirmed. There are no dates, venue, funding or registration yet, so nothing on this page books a flight or a place. Read [LOADOUT IRL](/docs/irl) for the event status.

Flying is optional. Not everyone needs to fly to take part.

## Flight cover {#cover}

Each flyer's ticket includes a **flight cover of $350** toward the return fare. This is a planned value.

## Grant hours for fares above the cover {#grants}

A fare above $350 can be covered with grant hours: **2 approved hours for every $10 above the cover**. Grant hours are extra approved hours on top of the 70 hours for the ticket.

| Return fare | Above the cover | Grant hours | Approved hours in total |
|---|---|---|---|
| $400 | $50 | 10 | 80 |
| $450 | $100 | 20 | 90 |
| $500 | $150 | 30 | 100 |
| $800 | $450 | 90 | 160 |

A flyer who doesn't reach the grant hours pays the gap themselves or from an outside pool.

## What is not covered yet {#not-covered}

- Fares are not priced yet, so the table above is an example, not a quote.
- Airport transfers, visas and onward travel are still being planned.
- Nothing on this page is a payment or a promise until the event is confirmed.
