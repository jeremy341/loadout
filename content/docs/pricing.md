Why might someone else pay less Ore for the same item? Their level in its track might be higher. Your track level gives you a permanent discount, and a matching Requisition can take another 10% off the base price. Here's how those savings work together.

## How a price is worked out

1. Find the **base Ore price** on the item.
2. Subtract your **related track discount**, which grows with your level.
3. For an eligible purchase, subtract **another 10% of the base price** if you use a matching [Requisition](/docs/requisitions).
4. Check the final total and the order terms before confirming.

Both discounts use the original price. Together they can't go above **25% of the base price**. We don't take the second discount from the smaller subtotal.

## An example

Say you're saving for a matching item listed at 1,000 Ore, and you have a Requisition saved up. Using it takes 100 Ore off that base price first:

| Line | Ore |
| --- | --- |
| Base price | 1,000 |
| Requisition (10%) | −100 |
| **Before your level discount** | **900** |

Your level discount then comes off the same 1,000 base price. At LV.15 that is another 150 Ore, so you'd pay 750 Ore in total, the most the two discounts can save. If you don't use the ticket yet, keep it for a later purchase. Requisitions only work on items with a **base price of at least 400 Ore**, so a smaller purchase can use your level discount while leaving the ticket untouched.

## When an item relates to two tracks

What if an item belongs to both Systems and Hardware? Use whichever of your related tracks gives you the better discount. You can't add two track discounts together on the same item.

## Can I buy outside my track? {#buying-outside-your-specialty}

Yes. You can spend Ore earned in any track on most prizes, so earning it through Hardware doesn't stop you buying something for Compute. The discount comes from your level in the item's related track. Some specialist items also require a particular level, which you'll still need even if you have enough Ore or a Requisition.

## Still to be confirmed
