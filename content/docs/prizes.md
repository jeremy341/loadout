The shop is where your projects can help you get equipment for the next build. Maybe you're after a soldering iron, a board to experiment on or a machine you can leave running. The Ore you earn from accepted projects goes toward that equipment, whether you're picking up a small tool or saving for a bigger upgrade.

## Does my track affect what I can buy? {#pricing}

For most prizes, you're free to spend Ore from any track. If you earned it designing a board but now want a domain, go ahead. What changes is the discount: that uses your level in the item's related track. Building more in one field makes its equipment cheaper, while leaving you free to try something else.

The permanent discount grows with your level, up to 15% at LV.15. Some specialist prizes can have their own level requirements; a discount doesn't skip those. [Pricing](/docs/pricing) explains how the final total is worked out.

## Field Requisitions {#requisitions}

Your level discount isn't the only saving. At certain track milestones you'll also earn a Field Requisition, which takes **another 10% of the base price** off one matching prize worth at least **400 Ore**. You can use one ticket on an order, alongside the permanent discount you've earned.

If you're saving for a printer, for instance, it could be worth keeping a ticket for that purchase. [Field Requisitions](/docs/requisitions) explains when you earn them and how they combine with your level discount.

## Custom Orders {#custom-orders}

Can't find the equipment you need? [Custom Orders](/docs/custom-orders) let you request an item outside the shop. Explain what you'll use it for and send a supplier link. If we can support the request, you'll get a quote to accept or decline, with the requirements and discounts shown.

## Availability

These are working catalog examples, with final stock and delivery arrangements still to come. [Program status](/docs/eligibility#where-things-stand) explains what has opened. Before accepting an order, read its price and delivery terms so you know exactly what you're agreeing to.
