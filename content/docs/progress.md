Approved work earns two things: Ore, which you spend, and Track XP, which you keep.

## Two kinds of progress {#balances}

| What | What it does | Can you spend it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ore | The currency for prizes. | Yes, once ordering opens. |
| Track XP | Your lasting level in Tools, Systems, Compute or Hardware. | No. |
| Era Points | The community's shared progress during an Era. | No. |

Getting more of one doesn't automatically give you more of the others.

## How an hour becomes Ore {#work}

**Ore = approved hours × your rating.** Other builders rate your project from 0 to 20, and the rating is how many Ore each hour pays.

| Rating | What it means | Ten hours pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Rough but honest: it exists, but it's unfinished or unclear. | 50 Ore |
| 10 | Solid: it works, it took real effort, and it's explained. | 100 Ore |
| 15 | Great: clearly above what most builders ship. | 150 Ore |
| 20 | Outstanding: the kind of thing you'd show an expert. | 200 Ore |

Twenty is the top of the scale. [Project review](/docs/review) explains what reviewers look at.

A project that wins the Era bonus (+10% on the approved Ore) adds it on top. There's no limit on how many hours or projects you can ship, as long as the hours are real.

## How an hour becomes Track XP

Reviewers assign Track XP to the fields your approved work demonstrates. Each project has one XP award, divided across those fields, so the same work doesn't receive a full award in several tracks. The exact equation and its quality weighting are among the [remaining decisions](/docs/eligibility#where-things-stand).

## Your field levels {#levels}

Each track has fifteen levels, and you keep them for good. Level 15 takes about **350 approved hours of solid work in one track**. If you work in two areas, you reach level 15 in both after about 700 hours, so spreading out is fine. It just takes longer.

Levels **3, 6, 9, 12 and 15** each give you one [Requisition](/docs/requisitions). At LV.15, you have **3,500 Track XP and a 20% discount**. The thresholds and discounts between those milestones are still being set.

Levels don't multiply the Ore a project earns.

## What stays with you

Your lifetime Track XP, your accepted projects and your Digital Loadout carry over through Seasons and Era changes. Competitive ranks belong to their own period.

The idea is to build up real skill over many projects, not to spend your XP or start over each season.
