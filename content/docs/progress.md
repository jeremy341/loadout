Once we've accepted your ship, you get two rewards: **Ore** to spend in the shop and **Track XP** that levels up the fields you worked in. You spend the Ore when you buy something, but the XP stays. As your level goes up, your discount on gear in that track grows, including on gear you buy later.

## What's the difference between Ore and XP? {#balances}

| What you earn | What happens to it |
| --- | --- |
| Ore | Goes into your balance. Buying a prize spends some of it. |
| Track XP | Goes into the tracks your work used. It stays when you buy something. |

There's also [Era Points](/docs/eras), which track the community's shared progress toward the next theme. They don't go into your shop balance or personal levels.

## How an hour becomes Ore {#work}

Every approved hour earns **10 Ore**. The rate is fixed:

```text
base Ore = approved hours × 10
```

So ten approved hours earn 100 Ore, and twenty earn 200. There's no multiplier, no peer score and no bonus on top at launch. The team checks your hours and decides whether to accept the ship; the rate itself doesn't change.

## How an hour becomes Track XP {#xp}

The team splits Track XP between the tracks your ship declares and uses. Designing a board and building a substantial service for it can contribute to Hardware and Systems, but the two tracks share one award. Listing both doesn't double your XP.

We haven't settled the XP equation, so don't predict an exact XP number. We do have a target for how long it should take to reach the top level.

## Your field levels {#levels}

Each track has **15 lifetime levels**. LV.15 is **2,750 Track XP**, with a target of about **275 approved hours of solid work in one track**. If you split the work equally between two tracks, reaching LV.15 in both is a target of about 500 hours altogether.

If the ship also qualifies for the [Era bonus](/docs/eras#bonus), add 10% of its base Ore award. A 100-Ore base award becomes 110 Ore. It adds no Track XP. How it combines with future Ore bonuses is still undecided.

Your permanent discount on related gear grows with your track level, reaching **15% at LV.15**. Along the way, levels **5, 8, 11 and 15** each give you a [Field Requisition](/docs/requisitions) for an extra 10% off one larger purchase.

We're still setting the intermediate XP thresholds and discount percentages. The milestones above don't imply a published percentage at every level, and leveling up doesn't change the Ore you earn per hour.

## Do my levels ever reset? {#what-stays-with-you}

No. Your Track XP, levels, accepted projects and Digital Loadout stay with you across Eras. Buying something spends Ore from your balance, but it won't take away the progress you made to earn it.

Each Era lasts two weeks and changes the community's shared theme. Neither sends your tracks back to level one.
