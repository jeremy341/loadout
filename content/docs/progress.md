Once we've accepted your ship, you get two rewards: **Ore** to spend in the shop and **Track XP** that levels up your project's track. You spend the Ore when you buy something, but the XP stays. As your level goes up, your discount on gear in that track grows, including on gear you buy later.

## What's the difference between Ore and XP? {#balances}

| What you earn | What happens to it |
| --- | --- |
| Ore | Goes into your balance. Buying a prize spends some of it. |
| Track XP | Goes into the track of the project you shipped. It stays when you buy something. |

There's also [Era Points](/docs/eras), which track the community's shared progress toward the next theme. They don't go into your shop balance or personal levels.

## How an hour becomes Ore {#work}

Every approved hour earns **10 Ore**. The rate is fixed:

```text
base Ore = approved hours × 10
```

So ten approved hours earn 100 Ore, and twenty earn 200. There's no multiplier, no peer score and no bonus on top at launch. The team checks your hours and decides whether to accept the ship; the rate itself doesn't change.

## How an hour becomes Track XP {#xp}

A project has exactly one track. You pick it when you create the project, and all the Track XP from a ship goes to that track. A board and the service that runs on it are two different projects if you want both fields to count, each with its own track.

Each approved hour gives **10 Track XP**, split across the tracks your ship uses. XP is a straight count of your approved hours, so the hours you've logged tell you your XP. Your level depends on your approved hours in one track, not on XP.

## Your field levels {#levels}

Each track has **20 lifetime levels**. LV.15 is a target of about **178 approved hours of solid work in one track**, LV.18 about **236 hours**, and LV.20 about **275 hours**. The Track XP figures for these levels are still being set, so this page doesn't give an XP number for them yet. If you split the work equally between two tracks, reaching LV.15 in both is a target of about 356 hours altogether.

If the ship also qualifies for the [Era bonus](/docs/eras#bonus), add 10% of its base Ore award. A 100-Ore base award becomes 110 Ore. It adds no Track XP. How it combines with future Ore bonuses is still undecided.

Your permanent discount on related gear grows with your track level, reaching **15% at LV.15** and **20% at LV.20**. Along the way, levels **5, 8, 11, 15, 18 and 20** each give you a [Field Requisition](/docs/requisitions) for an extra 10% off one larger purchase.

We're still setting the intermediate XP thresholds and discount percentages. The milestones above don't imply a published percentage at every level, and leveling up doesn't change the Ore you earn per hour.

## Do my levels ever reset? {#what-stays-with-you}

No. Your Track XP, levels, accepted projects and Digital Loadout stay with you across Eras. Buying something spends Ore from your balance, but it won't take away the progress you made to earn it.

Each Era lasts two weeks and changes the community's shared theme. Neither sends your tracks back to level one.
