As you level up a track, you'll earn **Field Requisitions**: one-use tickets that take an extra 10% off a matching item's base price. They work on top of your level discount, and you can hold onto one while you save for the gear you want.

## How do I get one? {#how-you-earn-one}

Each track awards a Requisition at **LV.5, LV.8, LV.11 and LV.15**. That's four per track. They all give the same **extra 10% discount**.

You get them by earning [Track XP](/docs/progress#levels) from your projects. When your level reaches one of those milestones, you earn the ticket. It doesn't cost Ore, and we don't subtract XP from your track to give it to you.

## What one does

Let's see what that ticket saves. It takes **10% of the item's base Ore price** off, in addition to your level discount in the matching track. Suppose you want an item that costs 1,000 Ore:

| Line | Ore |
| --- | --- |
| Base price | 1,000 |
| Requisition (10%) | −100 |
| **Before your level discount** | **900** |

Your level discount then comes off the same base price. Together, the two savings can reach at most **25%** of the base price. There's no Ore savings cap beyond that. The item's base price must be **400 Ore or more** for a ticket to apply.

## Using one

When you're ready to buy, check that the item's track matches your ticket, then look at the saving before confirming. For something that needs a custom quote, check that the quote allows a Requisition on that item.

Once you use it, that ticket is gone. Your level discount stays, though, so future purchases still benefit from the level you've reached. A ticket doesn't expire, so you can save up Ore for the purchase you have in mind.

## The limits

- Only **one Requisition per order**. You can't stack several tickets for a bigger reduction.
- The ticket belongs to you and can't be transferred. There's no coupon code to share.
- It reduces a purchase price; it doesn't add Ore to your balance.
- You still need the item's track and level requirements, and we must be able to buy and deliver it.
- If we cancel the order, the Requisition is returned to you.

[Pricing](/docs/pricing) covers the calculation. [Custom Orders](/docs/custom-orders) covers requests that need an individual quote, once they open.

Rare General Requisitions for exceptional community contributions are still being considered. They aren't an announced way to earn extra tickets.
