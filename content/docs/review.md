Review checks whether the project fits LOADOUT, whether its submitted work is valid, and how strong the approved technical contribution is.

## What reviewers check {#stages}

| Stage | Main question |
| --- | --- |
| Project fit | Does the technical core belong in LOADOUT? |
| Validity | Do the artifact, journals, time, and attribution support the submission? |
| Quality | How strong is the approved contribution across the four dimensions? |

A project can be useful and still be outside LOADOUT's focus. A relevant project can also need more evidence before its work is approved.

## Four quality dimensions {#quality}

| Dimension | What it asks |
| --- | --- |
| Originality | What is new in the implementation, approach, or constraints you tackled? |
| Technical Depth | What technical challenge did you solve, and how much do you understand? |
| Execution | Does the result work for its intended purpose? |
| Documentation | Can another builder understand, verify, or reproduce it? |

Quality informs the Bolt multiplier. Exact scoring calibration and payout values remain pending; these dimensions are not a published calculator.

## AI use and team projects {#authorship}

Reviewers need to understand what you built yourself. Declare AI assistance, borrowed code, dependencies, and each person's contribution.

[AI & team contributions](/docs/ai-teams) explains the intended attribution expectations.

## Feedback and revisions {#feedback}

Feedback should identify the decision, the eligible scope, and any missing evidence or improvements. A request for changes is not the same as a final acceptance.

If you revise a ship, explain what you changed and provide the evidence requested. Do not submit the same work twice to seek a different award.

## Questions about a decision

The planned appeal process should let builders question decisions about fit, approved work, quality, or track allocation. Its final submission route and timing are not yet published.

Keep the review reference and state which decision you are asking about. Use **#loadout-help** for clarification; it cannot promise a particular outcome.
