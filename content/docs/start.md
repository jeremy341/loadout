Got a tool you wish existed, a server you want to run, or a board you want to build? That's the sort of project you can bring to LOADOUT. As you build it, keep a journal of what you're doing, then ship a version other people can try. Once it passes review, we'll award **Ore** you can spend on gear and **Track XP** in the fields you worked in. So the project you've just finished can help you get what you need for the next one.

## Who it's for {#program}

LOADOUT is a You Ship, We Ship program for high-school students aged **13 to 18**. You can come with friends or build on your own, and your first project doesn't have to be huge. A useful command-line tool is a perfectly good place to start.

The four tracks are **Tools, Systems, Compute and Hardware**. They're broad enough for a debugger, a database component, a renderer or a sensor board, but the important part is the work you do yourself. If you're building an app, tell us about the interesting engineering inside it. [What can I build?](/docs/projects) is a question which will help you work out whether your idea fits.

## Your two Loadouts {#loadout}

You'll see us talk about two Loadouts. Your **Digital Loadout** is the collection of projects you've shipped and had accepted, so other builders can see what you've made. Those projects also earn Ore, which you can save for equipment. We call that equipment your **Physical Loadout**, because it's the stuff you'll actually use when you sit down to build again.

You don't have to start from scratch every time. Ship a first version, learn what needs work, then come back with an improvement. Each ship earns for its new approved hours, while your Track XP keeps adding up. As you level up a track, related gear gets cheaper.

## What an hour is worth {#hours}

How much would your project earn? That depends on the hours we can approve and the **Ore multiplier** it gets in review. Normal rates run from **1× to 20×**, with a target mean of **10×**. At 10×, each approved hour earns 10 Ore, so ten approved hours earn 100 Ore. A project that reaches **Great Signal** can earn up to **30× per approved hour**.

For something to aim at, the working catalog lists an ESP32 starter kit at 50 Ore. That would take five approved hours at 10×, before any discount. The [prize list](/docs/prizes) has the other examples; [Ore, XP & levels](/docs/progress) explains how the rewards work.

## How you join {#join}

For now, you can [RSVP for launch updates](https://rsvp.soon.it/loadout). We'll still need you to register when the program opens; the RSVP is how you keep up with it in the meantime. And if you join after the first Season, your own progress will carry into the next one. You won't have to start your levels over every time.
