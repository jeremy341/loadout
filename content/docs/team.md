Who's making all of this? There are four of us putting LOADOUT together: Jerry, Fazin, Netic and Kernel. The work goes from figuring out the program rules to building this site, developing the bots and looking after the Slack community. Here's who handles each part, with links if you'd like to see what else they've made.

## Jerry, lead organizer {#jerry}

Jerry leads LOADOUT and builds the whole website you're reading, from the UI to the animations and pixel art. Outside LOADOUT, he made [Poorup](https://poorup.tech), a multiplayer property-trading game you can play in your browser. Its server handles the game state and reconnecting players, with automated tests checking changes. If you're curious about how that works, you can look through the [public source](https://github.com/jeremy341/Poorup).

## Fazin / Wind, researcher and co-organizer {#fazin}

Fazin researches how LOADOUT should work and helps organize the program.

## Netic, developer and co-organizer {#netic}

Seen the mole on the homepage? That's Orry, who also has a job in Slack. Netic develops the bot that lets Orry answer program questions using these Docs and pass difficult questions to human helpers. You can see the work in the bot's [public repository](https://github.com/NeticYTOF/orry), under Netic's NeticYTOF account.

## Kernel, community manager {#kernel}

Kernel looks after the Slack community and develops Goose Lee, the duck you'll meet there. Alongside the bot, he works on community ideas and events so people have places to share what they're making and get to know the other builders.

## Hack Club {#hack-club}

Hack Club is a nonprofit community of teenagers who learn by making things. Its You Ship, We Ship programs reward projects with gear and other prizes. LOADOUT is being proposed as one of those programs; acceptance is still unconfirmed, as explained on the [status page](/docs/eligibility).

## Follow along {#follow}

You can read the [LOADOUT source](https://github.com/jeremy341/loadout), ask in [#loadout-help](https://app.slack.com/client/E09V59WQY1E/C0C6XBLM0M7), or open a GitHub issue if you've spotted a problem.

If you came looking for Orry and Goose Lee, they have [a story page of their own](/docs/characters).
