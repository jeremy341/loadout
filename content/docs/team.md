Four people are behind LOADOUT. Here's who we are and what we've made.

## Jerry, lead organizer {#jerry}

Leads LOADOUT and builds this site. Jerry also made [Poorup](https://poorup.tech), a browser-based real-time multiplayer property-trading game. It has an authoritative Node.js server, recovery when a player reconnects, automated tests and checks that run on every change. [See its code](https://github.com/jeremy341/Poorup).

## Fazin / Wind, researcher and co-organizer {#fazin}

Does the research behind how LOADOUT works. Fazin built Vey, the decision model that powers Orry: a small runtime that makes quick, structured decisions on a normal CPU, without generating text. It is published on Hugging Face at [fazinahamed/vey](https://huggingface.co/fazinahamed/vey).

## Netic, developer and co-organizer {#netic}

Builds Orry, our German mining mascot and Slack bot, which answers questions about the program from its own docs and hands the hard ones to human helpers. The code is public on GitHub under the NeticYTOF account, which has 14 public repositories. [See Orry's code](https://github.com/NeticYTOF/rivet).

## Kernel, community manager {#kernel}

Kernel is our community manager. He regularly comes up with community ideas and hosts community events, and is responsible for Slack and the community spaces around LOADOUT. Kernel is also responsible for Goose-lee, the community duck bot.

## Goose-lee, the community bot {#goose}

Say hello to Goose-lee, the duck who lives in the official #loadout Slack channel. Goose-lee is a community bot, not part of the LOADOUT program itself. Its job is to make the channel a friendlier place to hang out.

Goose-lee keeps a little pond of crumbs. You earn them by chatting, by getting duck reactions from people who say thanks, by answering a question that the person who asked marks as helpful, and when an organizer gives you a star. Crumbs add up to pond levels, from 1 up to 12 (Head Duck), and the levels carry on past that.

Once a week Goose-lee shows an organizer a top 10 of the pond. Nothing goes public until an organizer confirms it, and only people who chose to be shown appear in the Pond Report. The top 3 each week get custom rewards, and the top 3 of all time get extra custom rewards. You can check your own page any time with `/goose me`. You can look at someone else's stats only if they chose to share them with `/goose show`.

### Goose-lee's backstory {#goose-backstory}

This is fiction, for fun. None of it is program information.

Goose-lee is Orry's rival. Goose-lee was already at LOADOUT when Orry arrived, and Goose-lee won the heart of the mole Orry first learned to swim for. In the mining contest, Goose-lee keeps beating Orry, and Orry takes it very personally.

## Orry {#orry}

Orry is our German mining mascot, the ore-mining mole at the bottom of the homepage, and our Slack bot. He is a Maulwurf, a mole, and he works the ore: he digs down, pops up somewhere else along the bottom of the screen, and sniffs at anything new.

He keeps an eye on you as well. When your pointer comes near, he looks at it, and he follows it when he is in a good mood. Pet his head and he gets happy, and if you keep it up he gets blissful. Poke him and he is surprised, pick him up and he wriggles. Push him too far and he sulks. The button in the footer hides him for good, and the same button brings him back.

In Slack, Orry answers questions from the program's own docs, and he hands the hard ones to human helpers as tickets. Netic builds him.

### Orry's backstory {#orry-backstory}

This is fiction, for fun. None of it is program information.

Orry grew up poor in the Ruhrgebiet. When he had nothing, Hannes helped him, and Hannes became his mentor and closest friend. Orry is an Olympic swimming champion for moles. He first learned to swim to impress someone he had a crush on, who chose Goose-lee instead.

Jerry's salary brought Orry to LOADOUT. On his first day he found out that Goose-lee was already there, and he was shocked. He still competes with Goose-lee at mining, out in the open. When Goose-lee wins, he grumbles and starts training for a rematch.

Orry has a huge private ore stash, and he stays loyal to his closest friends. He is cheerful and a bit cheeky. He will happily bargain about that make-believe stash, but he always helps for real, and his greedy streak never turns into a real promise, fee or reward.

## Hack Club {#hack-club}

Hack Club is a nonprofit community of teenagers who learn by making. Its You Ship, We Ship programs offer prizes for projects. LOADOUT is a proposal in development, and Hack Club hasn't accepted it yet. See [current status](/docs/eligibility).
