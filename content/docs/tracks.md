LOADOUT has four lifetime tracks. They describe the fields in which your work develops technical depth.

## The four fields

| Track | What you might build | What to demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tools | A CLI, debugger, profiler, SDK, test system, or developer automation. | How it improves a builder's workflow. |
| Systems | A runtime, database component, protocol, deployment tool, or network service. | The behavior, constraints, and reliability of the system. |
| Compute | A renderer, inference engine, GPU workload, numerical implementation, or optimization. | Correctness and useful measurements under stated conditions. |
| Hardware | A PCB, embedded device, robot subsystem, FPGA design, or instrument. | Design decisions and evidence that the physical result works. |

## One project, several tracks

Choose a primary field and explain any additional technical work. Reviewers decide the final Track XP allocation based on the approved contribution.

For example, a device may combine firmware, electronics, and a profiling tool. That does not mean every team member receives the same allocation, or that the same work earns several complete awards.

## Why choose a track?

Track XP builds lasting progress in the fields you actually demonstrate. Related levels can improve field pricing and qualify you for some specialist prizes or Custom Orders.

[Track progress](/docs/progress) explains why levels differ from Bolts. [Pricing](/docs/pricing) explains how specialization relates to a purchase.

## Research is a mode

Research Mode can apply to any of the four tracks. It does not create a fifth track or replace the usual review.
