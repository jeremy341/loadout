# Staging posture (owner, 22 Nov 2026)

**Internal. Not public copy.** This record states why the platform is being built and what it is allowed to claim while it is being prepared.

## Purpose

The application is **not made for use**. It is being prepared for a future move onto Hack Club infrastructure. The immediate goal is to let the owner and co-organisers test it online, with the understanding that not everything works and some things are expected to stay broken.

## What that means in practice

- Treat every deployment as a staging environment, not a launch. Merging or deploying is not a service claim.
- Incomplete, stubbed or fixture-backed features are acceptable when labelled. A control that accepts input and does nothing must say so; it must not imply success.
- Live eligibility, dates, registration, stock, ordering, fulfilment, delivery and service claims still require confirmation. Show owner-approved working values as provisional; never present them as final.
- Public `content/docs/` copy does not change because of staging work. Plans and internal development records own the rules.
- The owner alone decides when the application stops being staging. Nothing in this record grants permission to announce a live service.

## Testing with co-organisers

Co-organisers can be given access to a staging deployment to check flows and give feedback. Feedback about broken or missing behaviour is expected and is not a release blocker. Report gaps in the owning plan or development record rather than treating them as production incidents.
