# How to start a YSWS

A YSWS ("you ship, we ship") is a community project where people build something and a sponsor pays for the results. This guide describes the path from idea to launch, written from one builder's experience. It's a community guide, not official Hack Club documentation. Ask the YSWS organisers when in doubt.

Slack links below point to the Hack Club Slack. You need to be a member to open them.

## 1. Find an idea

Pick something unique. Before you commit, check `#ysws-drafts` and `#ysws` for inspiration, and make sure you haven't accidentally copied another pitch or an existing YSWS.

## 2. Write a short pitch

Explain the idea clearly without boring people. Keep it under the message limit for `#ysws-drafts`. Other people's pitches are a good model for length and tone.

## 3. Build a website if people respond

If the pitch gets interest, start on a website. Build it even when replies are slow. A good site can draw in people who would ignore a plain pitch. Look at other YSWS websites for ideas. The community has a beginner template you can start from, and existing projects such as Pixl are useful references.

## 4. Pitch again with the website

Post the pitch again in `#ysws-drafts`, this time with the website. Wait for feedback. Expect criticism, and treat it as useful.

## 5. Find a sponsor

Post the project in other channels, such as `#present`, and wait for a sponsor to approach you. Finding one can take weeks or months. A very unique idea can get a sponsor much faster. Keep polishing the project while you wait.

## 6. Meet the sponsor

Once you have a sponsor, expect meetings about the YSWS and your goals. Answer confidently, and accept that you'll make mistakes while you learn.

## 7. Get permission to announce

If the sponsor agrees, you can announce the YSWS as sponsored. Agree on a launch date first, and don't rush it. To get posting permission for `#ysws`, send a DM to `@/cloud`.

## 8. Announce it

Write a clear announcement that describes the YSWS well and keep it short. Post it in `#ysws`.

## 9. Run reviews

Reviews and fulfilment are the two main jobs.

- Your sponsor should decide the rate you pay per hour, because they handle the money.
- If you have no review experience, ask for free reviewers by DM or through a reviewer form. You can also post in `#hc-opportunities`.
- Don't pay reviewers until you have a reasonable budget. Once you do, a rate of about 21 to 70 cents per review was a sensible range for this builder.

## 10. Fulfil rewards

If the sponsor gives you HCB permissions, you can spend the budget yourself. If they handle fulfilment, you have less to do. HCB is simple to learn.

## 11. Fulfil physical items

Physical items are more complicated than grants, but they're more fun. Order from a trusted shop, enter the recipient's address and phone number, and pay with HCB. Then send the recipient the tracking ID.

## 12. Fix a review backlog

If the review queue gets out of hand, hire more reviewers. Once the queue is stable, you can remove reviewers who aren't pulling their weight.

## Questions

Ask in `#ask-the-ysws-orgs`, or DM an experienced organiser.
