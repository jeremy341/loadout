## Stardance behavior and test evidence

- A countable Vote has four 1–9 values: originality, technicality, usability, and storytelling. Vote validation requires a reason of at least ten words, rejects self-votes, and enforces one voter per ship event. See app/models/vote.rb and test/models/vote_test.rb.
- Assignment is a separate model and matchmaker. Twelve countable votes are required to leave the pool and enter payout review; the first twelve ordered by creation time and ID are snapshotted. A skipped assignment can be replaced. The matchmaker excludes the voter’s own projects, previous votes/assignments, skipped projects, and reported projects. It favors old and near-threshold ships, gates assigned ship hours at up to three times a reviewer’s approved hours until they have 50 hours, and can sample up to 50 paid ships for a reviewer with negative vote balance. No dedicated matchmaker test file was found. See app/models/post/ship_event.rb, app/models/vote/assignment.rb, and app/models/vote/matchmaker.rb.
- Reviewer-quality signals include assignment view counts/timestamps, time-to-vote, and whether the reviewer opened the demo and repository. Vote reasons are stored and embedded for later analysis. Votes are unique per voter/ship, self-votes are rejected, and a countable vote needs four integer scores from 1–9 plus a reason of at least ten words. See app/models/vote.rb, app/models/vote/assignment.rb, app/models/vote/event.rb, and app/jobs/vote/cache_reason_embedding_job.rb.
- Each dimension uses the median of the selected votes. The overall score is the mean of available dimension medians. Percentiles use midrank: (count below + half the count equal) / population * 100, rounded to two decimals; an all-tied population gets 50. This is not explicit outlier removal. See app/models/post/ship_event/payouts.rb.
- The peer payout curve clamps percentile to 0–100, converts it to a curve with exponent 1.745427173 between configured low/high dollars per hour, multiplies by tickets_per_dollar (5.0 in config/game_constants.yml), then rounds the multiplier to six decimals. Base payout rounds hours × multiplier; a blessed verdict adds 20%, a cursed verdict halves it, and neutral leaves it unchanged. Hardware is a separate flat 5 Stardust/hour path or reviewer override. These formulas and currencies are not LOADOUT policy.
- Before lock, payout_preview reports an estimate, vote count, open-review state, deadline, pending flags, and blockers; changes to votes, time, score population, or verdict can change that estimate. At lock, the selected vote IDs, score, percentile, hours, multiplier, curve version, and blessing are stored. The 24-hour review period follows; final payout remains unset until the window expires or is accepted early. See app/models/post/ship_event/payouts.rb and app/models/post/ship_event.rb.
- A pending vote flag blocks issue. Accepted flags discard the rating, record an event, clear the snapshot, and reopen voting. Rejected flags record an event, charge the flagger 5 Stardust, and force issue. Project members and admins can flag a counted rating during the review window; admin resolution is separately authorized. No generalized post-payout appeal path was found in the audited routes. See app/controllers/votes/flags_controller.rb, app/controllers/admin/vote_flags_controller.rb, app/controllers/admin/vote_flags/approvals_controller.rb, app/controllers/admin/vote_flags/rejections_controller.rb, and test/controllers/vote_flags_controller_test.rb.
- The final issue runs under a lock/transaction, writes payout and a ledger row together, and is idempotent. The admin payout review list/detail itself is read-only. See app/models/ledger_entry.rb, app/controllers/admin/payout_reviews_controller.rb, app/policies/admin/payout_review_policy.rb, app/views/admin/payout_reviews/, and the controller/policy tests.
- Median aggregation limits one rating’s influence but is not explicit outlier rejection. No generalized post-payout appeal route was found in the audited paths. Auto-discard behavior cannot be verified because Secrets::VoteAutoDiscarder is absent.
- Relevant behavior tests: test/models/vote_test.rb; test/models/post/ship_event_test.rb; test/controllers/vote_flags_controller_test.rb; test/jobs/vote/auto_discard_job_test.rb; test/controllers/admin/payout_reviews_controller_test.rb; test/policies/admin/payout_review_policy_test.rb. They cover self-vote/duplicate/score validation, median/percentile and curve bounds, count threshold, vote-deficit hold, lock and immutability, idempotency, accepted/rejected flags, auto-discard delegation, and admin/NDA-helper access.
- The core records are Vote (unique user/ship index and score/reason/telemetry columns), Vote::Assignment (unique user/ship and assignment state), Vote::Event (flag/discard events), Post::ShipEvent (vote-ID array and versioned score/payout snapshot fields), and LedgerEntry. Their associations and constraints are in app/models/vote.rb, app/models/vote/assignment.rb, app/models/vote/event.rb, app/models/post/ship_event.rb, app/models/ledger_entry.rb, and db/schema.rb.

## Product boundary

LOADOUT uses its four categories as descriptive dimensions, not scores. Peer review, the percentile payout, and the flag window were removed on 22 Oct 2026. Stardance informs mechanics only; its currency, category names, Hardware exception, and source code are not adopted.
