# Rewards and recognition: Toolbelt, makers and Flagships — proposal

> Status: **current decisions, 14 Nov 2026.** The full Toolbelt rules are in [toolbelt.md](toolbelt.md). This file holds the Flagship award and the General Requisition, which only comes through a Flagship. Ore values are for [Plan 02](../02-economy-and-rewards.md) to confirm. Older checkpoint and pool drafts have been removed.

## 1. Toolbelt rewards (summary)

- **Builder who equips a tool/project:** **50 Ore once per tool/project**, after the significant-use gate passes (the equipping project needs 25 approved hours).
- **Maker of the tool/project:** **10 Ore per verified user** who passes the gate, paid as each user passes it, capped at **500 Ore per tool/project per season**.
- **Track XP:** none from equipping. Track XP is 10 per approved hour.
- **Recognition:** a "Keystone" tile for the most-equipped tool/project in each track (kept unless you drop it).
- No Usage Milestones, no badges, no checkpoints, and no seasonal pool.

## 2. Flagship: the standout award

A Flagship is an accepted tool/project or project that the **reviewer** judges to be an outstanding example. There is no fixed number per season.

**What a Flagship gives:**
- A featured slot on the homepage and in Explore for the season.
- A season trophy on the maker's profile and a Flagship label on the project page.
- **One General Requisition** for the builder. This is the only way to earn a General Requisition.

**What it does not give:** Ore, extra Track XP or a higher rate.

**Reviewer checklist (draft, to confirm):** the reviewer scores each of these as "clear", "partly" or "not":
1. **Technical depth:** how complex the work is, and how much of it the builder did themselves.
2. **Impressiveness:** whether the result does something hard or rare for a 13–18 year old.
3. **Execution:** whether it works, is tested, and can be run by someone else.
4. **Documentation:** whether the README and journal let a stranger follow the work.
5. **Originality:** whether the idea or approach is the builder's own.
6. **Use (for tools):** whether other builders use it, through Toolbelt or in public.

A Flagship needs **clear** on at least four of the six, including depth or impressiveness. The reviewer writes a short reason, as with every review decision.

## 3. General Requisition

- A General Requisition is a second ticket. It works in **any track** and stacks with the track discount and one Field Requisition.
- It is worth **10%** of the base price, on an item of **400 Ore or more**, one per order.
- **Earned only through a Flagship**, one per Flagship. So the number of General Requisitions is the number of Flagships.
- The combined cap with LV.20, a Field Requisition and a General Requisition is **40%**.
- The shop's requisition preview now supports General Requisitions. Holding a ticket and applying it in a real order still needs a ticket record, which isn't built yet.
